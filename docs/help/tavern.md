# Tavern

The Tavern is where you recruit **AI-controlled character allies** to travel and
fight alongside you. It offers both generated adventurers and eligible characters
from your other saves.

[Back to Help index](index.md)

## How to play

### Generated and saved recruits

- **Generated adventurers** have their own race, guilds, stats, alignment, and
  equipment, tuned to roughly match the **original party owner's** power (the
  character who started the party). Offers belong to that owner and refresh
  after an interval of time. Switching control to another party member does not
  change whose power or roster is used. Hiring costs bank gold, based on the
  recruit's equipment; dismissal does not refund it.
- **Saved characters** have no recruitment fee. They retain their actual guild
  levels, stats, equipment, inventory, and separate bank rather than being
  rebuilt to match the party owner. Their equipment is shown with its existing
  identification limits. Normal town recovery or death costs can still apply
  when bringing them into town.
- Only eligible characters with compatible alignments are offered. Characters
  already controlled or hired elsewhere are unavailable. A saved recruit's own
  personal allies and monsters stay inactive; they do not join your active party.
- **Dismissal** releases a saved character with their current progress and
  possessions intact, allowing them to become available for recruitment again.
  The original party owner remains in the party and cannot be dismissed.

### Party limits and switching

| Mode | Character allies | Monster slots |
|------|------------------|---------------|
| Solo | Up to 3 | Up to 8 |
| Co-op | Up to 1 per player | Up to 4 per player |

The character you control occupies the player position, not an ally slot.
Purchased and charmed monsters, plus any carried dead monster body, use the
separate [companion slots](companion-store.md).

Entering co-op keeps the first eligible members within each limit and **parks**
the excess. Parked members are retained but do not act or receive rewards.
Returning to solo restores eligible parked members within the larger limits.
Restored monster bodies still follow the
[one-body limit and discard warning](companion-store.md#carried-bodies-and-parked-companions).

Use **Switch to this character** on an eligible saved recruit's Tavern card,
or the [town name dropdown](town.md#switching-characters). In the dungeon, the
saved ally's menu offers the same switch. Only living, present, active saved
members of your own party are eligible; generated adventurers cannot be
controlled. Switching changes the played character, not party ownership or
monster slots, and grants no extra combat turn.

### AI, rewards, and recovery

- While AI-controlled, allies attack, defend, cast learned spells, and support
  the party using their [behavior and spell permissions](character-sheet.md#ally-ai-and-inspection).
  They do not initiate item spells or use up consumables for you.
- **Dungeon gold** is shared among the real characters participating in that
  encounter. Each receives their own carried share, banked on returning to town;
  generated adventurers' shares are expenses instead. A party in a separate
  co-op encounter does not receive that encounter's income.
- **Town sales** first account for generated-ally expenses, then divide the
  proceeds among eligible real characters' own banks. A saved recruit is a
  recipient, not another generated-ally fee. Living ally support can also lower
  store identification fees.
- **Lost allies** remain individually recoverable. Return to town, revisit
  the exact cell where an ally was left, or use **Soul Search / Retrieve Soul**
  to find or recover a selected lost member. Monster companions are not left
  behind by these ally-separation events.
- **Downed allies** can be revived in the dungeon. Generated adventurers recover
  when brought back to town. A saved character returned unrevived instead goes
  through normal character death/recovery, with penalties and costs paid from
  that character's own funds, not free generated-ally resurrection.

**Tips:**

- Pick an ally that complements your hero — a caster or healer covers gaps a
  melee build can't, and vice versa.
- Use [Town Exchange](character-sheet.md#town-exchange) to move items and bank
  gold between real party members without changing who controls them.

## Differences from the original

- **Autonomous adventurer recruitment.** The Tavern, generated allies, and hiring
  your other saved characters are remake features. Generated recruits charge a
  fee; saved recruits do not. Neither uses a Charisma hiring roll.
- **Separate character and monster rosters.** Character allies do not consume
  monster slots. Solo's three allies/eight monsters and co-op's one ally/four
  monsters per player use the mode-dependent limits above.
- **Live control switching.** Play one real character at a time while the others
  use AI, retaining their own progression and possessions.

# Companion Store (Confinement)

The Companion Store — the Confinement — is where you **buy and sell companions**
(monsters that fight at your side) and manage the ones you own.

[Back to Help index](index.md)

## How to play

The screen works like the [General Store](general-store.md), with a buy list, a
detail panel, and a sell list — but the goods are creatures, not equipment.

- **Buy a companion.** Pick a monster from the stock to recruit it as an ally. Each
  stocked creature-and-alignment bucket is listed separately, and prices scale
  with how powerful the creature is. There are **eight monster slots in solo**
  and **four per player in co-op**: purchased creatures, monsters you've charmed
  in the dungeon, and any carried dead companion body share that limit.
  Character allies recruited at the Tavern use separate slots.
- **Sell a companion.** Hand a companion you own back to the Confinement for gold.
- **Bind.** Pay to raise a companion's **bind level** by 4, up to a maximum of
  12, strengthening your hold on it so it's less likely to slip away from you. A
  creature that joined you of its own free will can't use this paid Bind service.
- **Realign.** For a fully identified companion, pay to change its **alignment**
  (Good, Neutral, or Evil) — useful for matching an ally to your hero.
- **Identify.** Pay to reveal more about a companion you don't fully know yet.

Newly bought companions are already fully identified. Companions recruited or
charmed in the dungeon can be only partly known, so identify those if you want
the full picture.

Your companions act on their own in combat.

### Carried bodies and parked companions

You can carry **only one dead monster companion body at a time**, and it uses
one monster slot.

**Take care when restoring parked companions:** if more than one body becomes
active, the game keeps the first in roster order and **discards the additional
bodies**. This can happen if a body was parked when entering co-op and you
collected another during co-op. Returning to solo increases your monster slots,
but does not increase the one-body limit.

### Binding and contentment

Owned-companion details show a **Bind level** or, for voluntary companions,
**Content level**, with a qualitative stability assessment. Dungeon rows show
matching faces: green for Completely/Excellently, gold for Fairly/Marginally,
and red for Weakly/Very Weakly. A separate lock means **Bound for life**.
Ordinary Completely bound is not permanent; binding can still drift later.

Learned **Control, Restrain, and Domination** are cast from the selected
companion's dungeon context menu, not the generic Cast list or spell hotbar.
These spells can target eligible living companions with nonzero binding or
contentment, including voluntary companions. Binding items, including Life
Domination, use their own item-use target picker.

An ally's **Charm** capability controls automatic hostile-monster recruitment
and useful binding maintenance outside hostile combat. It does not prevent
you from choosing a legal manual binding spell.

## Differences from the original

- **Mode-dependent companion limits.** Solo supports eight monster slots;
  co-op allows four per player. Excess monsters are parked, not sold or
  discarded, when switching to co-op, and can return within the solo limit.
  The one-body restoration exception above still applies. Tavern character
  allies remain separate from these slots.

The stock, pricing, binding, realigning, and identification all follow the
original game's confinement rules.

Dejenol Restoration Project

A fan remake of Mordor: The Depths of Dejenol (1995), modernized and written in Rust. You bring your own copy of the original game; the remake supplies new code and a rebalance on top.

It started as a fork of matteo-prosperi/DungeonsOfDejremake. That project publishes no source, so all the code here is new. Source: silvariasereneblossom/DungeonsOfDejremake.

Design pillars

  • Modern RPG mechanicsModern mechanics come first. Strict faithfulness to 1995 does not.
  • Corpse recoveryWhen a character dies, the body stays in the dungeon for someone to recover.
  • A party of your own charactersYour other characters join the party under AI control.
  • Flatter XP, deeper rewardsLevelling takes far less XP, and deeper floors pay more.
  • Casters get to castCasters get far more SP, and SP potions drop nearly as often as healing potions.
  • Fair boxes earlyMagically locked boxes are rare on floors 1-5 and get more common deeper down.
  • Original art and musicOriginal-game art and SoundFont-rendered MIDI music, extracted on your machine and never redistributed.
  • Everywhere, in RustDesktop and web first, mobile later.

The rebalance in numbers

The rebalance is a patch file, patches/modern.toml, applied on top of the imported original tables. Every value is a first-pass placeholder to tune during play-testing. Any other patches/*.toml overrides it key by key.

KnobValueMeaning
XP to next level120 × level1.6Far flatter than the original curve.
Depth bonus+15% per floorFloor 10 pays 2.35× the XP.
Catch-up bonus+10% per level, cap +100%For party members below the party's highest level.
Max SP gain2.5×Multiplier on the original max-SP gain per level.
SP regen0.5% per stepOf max SP, in the dungeon. Resting in town still refills.
SP potion drops0.85 × healingDrop weight relative to healing potions.
Magic lock chance1% → 25%1% on floors 1-2, 2.5% by floor 5, 8% on floor 6, 25% from floor 10 on.

Tech stack

PieceWhat it does
crates/rulesHeadless game rules and the tuning layer, with no window and fully testable. It includes the original-table parser mdr.rs.
crates/extractImports MORDOR11.ZIP, a .WIP archive, or an install folder into gamedata/.
crates/gameA thin egui frontend.
patches/TOML rebalance layers, merged key by key.

It's a single Cargo workspace in Rust. To try it with the bundled shareware:

cargo run -p ddj-extract -- tests/fixtures/MORDOR11.ZIP
cargo run -p ddj-game

Bring your own data

This repo and its builds contain no original-game content. You import your own copy, and the importer extracts the data tables, MIDI music and sounds into gamedata/ on your own machine. That folder is gitignored and never redistributed. The remake's changes ship only as patch files that apply on top of those tables.

The bundled shareware covers floors 1-3. Point the importer at a retail copy for all 15.
